# <Color id="gold">Iron Furnace</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="iron_furnace" scale="2" />

  A non-electric furnace that squeezes **12 items per coal**, 50% more than vanilla.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<RecipeFor id="iron_furnace" />

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

* Uses any vanilla furnace fuel.
* Exists specifically to bridge the gap before electricity. Once you have the <ItemLink id="powered_furnace" />, the Iron Furnace becomes obsolete. It needs no power and no progression.
* Cannot accept upgrades.

Drag an item from **JEI** onto the input or fuel slot to set a persistent ghost filter. Ghosts do not create items. With an empty cursor, right-click an empty ghost, or Shift-right-click a filled locked slot, to remove the filter. Matching includes item data, and hoppers obey these filters too.
